Editor's Notes

Destined for cocktail hour, weddings, and beyond, our signature Silk-Satin Gala Maxi Dress is the perfect way to celebrate any special occasion in style and elegance.

Cut from sleek silk-satin, the Gala Dress exudes timeless elegance with its flattering curved empire waistline that flows into a sweeping maxi skirt. A true statement piece, the double-flared pagoda sleeves and flared hem bring a playful, dramatic touch to this stunning design.

Printed in our very own cutting-edge Liberty fabric mill in northern Italy, this particular style of dress showcases the Madeleine print - named after Madeleine Lawrence, a gifted artist who worked for the Silver Studio during the 1930s, this design spotlights wild anemone clusters exuding radiance, romance, and finesse.

Composition & Care

100% Silk
